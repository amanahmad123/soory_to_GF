A Letter Just For You 💌

💌 Written with love, just for you 🌷

My Dearest Love, ❤️

I don't know the perfect way to start this letter, but I know that I want to be honest with you. I am truly sorry if my words or actions hurt you. You never deserved to feel unimportant, unheard, or less loved because of something I did. 🥺

Sometimes I can be silly, stubborn, or completely clueless. 😭😂 But I never want my mistakes to make you question how much you matter to me.

You are someone I care about deeply. Your happiness matters to me. Your feelings matter to me. Your opinions matter to me, even when I don't immediately understand them.

I know an apology is not magic. It cannot instantly erase hurt feelings, and I don't expect you to forget everything just because I said sorry. I want to listen, understand, learn, and show you through my actions that I mean what I say. 🤍

If you need time, I will respect that. If you want to talk, I want to listen without interrupting. And if there is something I need to change, I want to make a genuine effort.

I miss the little things: the smiles, the silly conversations, the random laughter, and those moments when even doing nothing together feels special. 🌸

I am not asking you to be happy before you are ready. I am simply asking for the chance to talk honestly when you feel comfortable.

You deserve to be treated with care, not just today while I am apologising, but in the ordinary moments that follow too. ❤️

So, from the bottom of my heart, I am sorry. No pressure, no excuses, just a sincere apology and a promise to try to do better.

With all my love and one very big hug, 🤗💗
